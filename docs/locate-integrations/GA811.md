# GA811 — integration status

**Status: not yet confirmed. Adapter interface only; no automated retrieval.**

Georgia 811 (Georgia Utilities Protection Center). Vantara must not scrape it,
bypass a CAPTCHA, or drive the public site with a browser. Until GA811 confirms
an authorized method, `GA811Adapter` implements the interface and returns
`UNSUPPORTED` from its retrieval calls.

## What we need from GA811

Ask, in this order of preference:

1. **Positive Response API / web service.** Ticket lookup and member responses
   by ticket number. REST or SOAP both fine.
2. **Authorized machine-readable feed** — a scheduled export of responses for
   tickets where we are the excavator of record.
3. **Authenticated remote-access account** with documented programmatic use.
4. **Written confirmation** that automated retrieval of our own tickets from an
   existing interface is permitted, with any rate limits stated.

Also ask for:

- Sandbox or test credentials
- Rate limits and acceptable polling frequency
- Whether responses can be pushed to us instead of polled
- The authoritative list of member codes and response codes, with meanings
- Whether ticket lifetime, renewal window and update rules are exposed, or
  whether we must compute them

## What we already know

- Ticket numbers appear in confirmation email as a long numeric string.
- Confirmation email carries: ticket number, revision, type, lat/lng, county,
  street, cross street, work type, called-in date, work-may-begin date,
  update-by date, expiry, locate instructions, and the member list with
  facility types.
- One real GA811 email has been parsed end to end by `parseLocateText` in
  `src/lib/locate-chat.ts`, including six members and coordinates.

## Unknowns

- Whether positive responses are available programmatically at all.
- Whether an excavator can query tickets they did not call in (relevant when a
  subcontractor holds the ticket on a Fortitude project).
- Exact expiry and renewal arithmetic.
